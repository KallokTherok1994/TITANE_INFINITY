## 📝 Lessons Learned

### What Worked Well ✅
1. **Phased approach** (0→1→2-3→4-6D→Final→7→Validation)
2. **Zero suppression policy** (1,428 files archived, not deleted)
3. **Code-first documentation** (100% factualité, zero speculation)
4. **Validation-driven** (test before Phase 8+, not after)
5. **Role-based navigation** (Getting Started hub for all personas)

### What Could Be Improved ⚠️
1. **Earlier validation** (could have validated after Phase 6D)
2. **Automated metrics** (manual tracking initially, automate Phase 1-3)
3. **i18n planning** (should have considered EN from Phase 0)

### What to Maintain 🎯
1. **Quality standards** (8.5/10, ≥8/10 target)
2. **Factualité FIRST** (zero speculation, code-based)
3. **Zero suppression** (archive, never delete)
4. **Validation cadence** (test after every major phase)
5. **Community-driven** (external contributors, feedback loops)
