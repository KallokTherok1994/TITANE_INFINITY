# 08_RESIDUAL_RISKS

Residual risks:
1. Workspace cleanliness target cannot be deterministically enforced without a single declared proof-pack tracking policy.
2. Future sessions may oscillate between `commit` and `keep-untracked` behavior.
3. Large untracked proof artifacts can pressure local storage and confuse precheck expectations.

Risk owner needed:
- Governance authority for docs/certification policy.
