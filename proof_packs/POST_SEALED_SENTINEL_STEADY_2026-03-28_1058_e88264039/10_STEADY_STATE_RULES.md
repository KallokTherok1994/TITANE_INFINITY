# Post-Sentinel Steady-State Rules

- Product scope stays closed unless a fresh proven trigger appears.
- Archive loss remains historical.
- Optional external backup may enrich archive only.
- Optional backup alone cannot reopen product scope.
- Future steady-state prompts must start from current sentinel baseline.
- Future reopen requires a fresh proven trigger.
- No generic “fix all” prompt allowed from this baseline.
- Governance/proof-only contradictions may be auto-corrected if minimal and safe.
